# StudyPulse

**FOCUS. REST. HYDRATE. REPEAT.**

StudyPulse is a premium, minimalist AMOLED desk watch and productivity companion built for focused study sessions, healthy eye care, and regular hydration.

## Features

- **Minimalist AMOLED Desk Watch**: Distraction-free digital desk clock showing clear date, time, active study session ring, and instant session start.
- **20-20-20 Eye Care Engine**: Gentle reminders and full-screen eye rest breaks every 20 minutes to reduce digital eye strain.
- **Smart Hydration Reminders**: Configurable water break alerts with quick confirmation and snooze options.
- **Custom Study Timers**: Support for Pomodoro, 50/10, custom countdown intervals, and free-flow stopwatch study modes.
- **Comprehensive Planner & Subjects**: Organize study tasks by custom color-coded subjects and granular topics.
- **Productivity Statistics**: Track daily focus streaks, weekly hours, subject breakdowns, and session histories.
- **Data Privacy & Export**: Completely offline Room persistence with JSON backup, CSV exports, and formatted study logs.
- **AMOLED-First Aesthetics**: Pitch-black canvas (`#000000`), warm cream text (`#F5F5F0`), golden-orange study accents (`#FFA640`), eye-care green (`#00D675`), and hydration blue (`#00B4FF`).

## Brand Identity
