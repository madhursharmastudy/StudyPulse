# DeskPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

DeskPulse is a study timer and desk watch app with eye-care and water reminders, a study planner and statistics.

## Data we collect
DeskPulse does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics in the app.

## Data stored on your device
The app stores your subjects, topics, study sessions, break records, planner items, settings and app logs only on your own device. This data is not sent to the developer or to any third party by the app.

## Permissions
- Notifications: to show study, eye-rest and water reminders.
- Exact alarms: to deliver reminders at the correct time.
- Vibration and wake lock: to alert you when a timer or break ends.

## Backup and sharing
If Android device backup is turned on, your device may back up the app data to your own Google account under Google's terms. If you copy or share your log or statistics text from the app, it goes only where you choose to send it.

## Children
DeskPulse does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data
You can delete all study data from inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
